# Harness distillation

An op produces three separable things, and only the deliverable ships; the lessons and the record are distilled forward.

## The rule

When an op closes, its deliverable, its lessons, and its record are kept apart. The deliverable is what the project wanted; the lessons feed the next op's starting line; the record is the durable history. Conflating them ships noise or loses memory.

## Instrument

The code is the mechanism; this leaf is the reason, so the two never drift. Enforced by
`alpaca/package.py` (op-close package) and `alpaca/historian.py`.
