# Alpaca NAPALM doctrine

Adapted from the upstream doctrine identified by BUNDLE-MANIFEST.json. This copy contains no inherited owner grant or operation history. Use available native operator tools and the parent model unless the current owner selects another model. Bound staffing by available concurrency and the current authorized budget. Store all operation artifacts under `.alpaca/napalm/`. Independent review must be reported as unavailable when separate reviewers cannot run.

## §0 - INVOCATION CONTRACT + G0 ROUTE: Napalm-vs-Nuclear is decided, not assumed

**"Run napalm on X"** = intake → **G0 control-pass** (**G0** names the route step; **N0**, §7, is the gate that
checks it) → route by the **breadth × stakes** 2×2 → convene the council (§1) → freeze the coverage map (§3) →
parallel BFS sweep → one round of different-lens cross-exam (§4) → fold (consensus/split/escalate) → hand hot
spots UP to Nuclear (§6) → converge by coverage+consensus (§5) → one honest report (§9). Owner silence = defaults
(§Defaults). **No owner turn is required before the sweep or before the report.**

**No council exists until G0 rules the route from disk.** The Convener does the opening survey directly and lists
bare gaps - exactly as Nuclear's control-pass does - then reads two axes off it:

- **SURFACE BREADTH** - one facet, or many independent facets each best judged by a different discipline?
- **COST-OF-WRONG-VERDICT (countable proxy - every route input a gate re-derives must be countable, LAW-COUNT):**
  **expensive = ≥1 control-pass gap at HIGH OR a one-way-door line named in the control-pass**; else
  cheap-to-be-wrong - a slightly-less-proven finding is an acceptable *re-decide*. (Backstopped by the mis-route
  banner, §7.)

| | cheap-to-be-wrong | expensive-to-be-wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **narrow** | **T0 do-it** - control-pass IS the deliverable, no council | **NUCLEAR** - depth-shaped; Napalm declines, drill from the start |
| **wide** | **NAPALM** - sweep the surface | **NAPALM first**, then escalate the hot spots to Nuclear |

The **same control-pass gap list feeds both engines** - Napalm reads it as *facets to cover*, Nuclear as *gaps
to drill*. The route is re-derivable from the frozen control-pass (LAW-COUNT). A subject with one live facet is
**not a council** - convening one anyway is a proportionality error caught at N0.

**Intake (six fields; ask ≤1 question, only for an unguessable high-stakes fork):** mission · subject (READ-ONLY)
· fence · **domain** {security · code · decision · research · composite} (drives the roster template, §2) ·
done-bar · **budget** (seats/bytes; rounds are structurally fixed at 2, §8; §Defaults apply if unstated -
INV-C11 inherited).

## §1 - THE THREE RANKS + MODEL MAP: flat by design

Napalm fields exactly **THREE ranks** - the flatness is the whole point; a fourth rank is a step back toward
Nuclear.

| Rank | Model | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| **CONVENER** (senior, solo) | inherited model | G0 + spotter + orchestrator, fused: control-passes and route-justifies, decomposes the surface into facets, derives the roster, freezes each seat's lens-charter and the cross-exam pairing, declares the budget. **Sole spawner · sole serial writer of the event log** (Nuclear's orchestrator invariant, preserved). Never judges primary content; may read a gate condition off a frozen artifact (count + compare, never classify). |
| **COUNCIL EXPERTS** (one per seat) | inherited model | Each authors a bare-claim memo on its **exclusive facet**, provenance-or-die, in parallel. Also plays **cross-examiner** for its paired peer. |
| **SYNTHESIZER** (the judge-analog) | inherited model | Folds the memos; stamps each finding `CONCURRED / SPLIT / ESCALATE`; writes the coverage+consensus tally + the dissent ledger; produces escalation packets. |

**Two standing ROLES (never ranks - a rank for either would re-inflate toward Nuclear, LAW-RATCHET):**
- **CROSS-EXAMINER** - every expert plays it for its paired peer (§4).
- **FRAME-CRITIC** - **one bounded reading seat** (inherited model) that reads the **subject + mission ONLY** - never the
  coverage map, roster, or charter - and asks: *is this the wrong decomposition? what facet does no seat own?*
  Its output goes **RAW to the owner, never folded** (Nuclear's frame-critic, in role form). It is the one head
  **outside the Convener's frame.** **v1.1: its partition objection, when filed, carries a recorded N1
  disposition - `owned` / `escalated` / `owner-waived-on-record` - never merely spawned and printed** (§7 N1).

*(Chain-composition is a conditional PASS, not a role and not a rank - §3.)*

**Model map** mirrors Nuclear: inherited model where judgment and adversarial lens are load-bearing (Convener, experts,
frame-critic); inherited model for the fold; inherited model permissible **only** for a mechanical coverage-instrument seat that
**measures**, never judges.

## §2 - ROSTER SELECTOR: subject area/context → distinct-lens seats

The Convener maps **area/context → seats** - what makes the breadth real rather than *N copies of one reviewer*.
Four re-derivable steps:

1. **CLASSIFY** the domain from the frozen control-pass and pull the matching **roster TEMPLATE**:
   - **security:** external-attacker · authz/trust-boundary · appsec/injection-sink · crypto/key-mgmt ·
     supply-chain/build · secrets/IaC · privacy/data-governance · insider/business-logic · detection/IR
   - **code:** correctness · API-contract · perf/lifecycle · maintainability/structure · test/verification-honesty ·
     security/trust-boundary · architecture/dependency-integrity · operability
   - **decision:** user-advocate · business/P&L · eng-cost/feasibility · risk/compliance · ethics/trust&safety ·
     ops/on-call · evidence/data · devil's-advocate
   - **research:** primary-source-scout · methodologist · mechanism-theorist · null/replication-hunter ·
     adjacent-discipline-importer · bibliometric-surveyor · genealogist · construct-critic · practitioner
2. **DECOMPOSE** the actual surface into facets and bind each template seat to the facets that **exist** - a
   template seat with no facet is **not convened** (filler forbidden).
3. **ADD SWING SEATS** only on demand - never as padding; an added seat must **name the facet the base roster
   missed** (LAW-RATCHET).
4. **FREEZE** the roster and stamp each seat a charter with **four orthogonality teeth**: a distinct **discipline**,
   a **disjoint owned facet** (LAW-PARTITION), a distinct **hunted failure**, and a distinct **required evidence
   slice** (a seat may not cite a peer's evidence as primary). Then assign the cross-exam pairing (§4).

**Facet granularity is gated (LAW-RATCHET):** at N0 each facet binds to a control-pass gap or a named template
lens; a facet finer than the template lenses carries the added-seat burden - name what it uniquely owns.
Sizing: 3 seats (small) to 7–9 (broad), within budget.

## §3 - COUNCIL FORMATION + THE FROZEN COVERAGE MAP + PARALLEL BFS SWEEP

**LAW-PARTITION (coverage map) - canonical home.** Before any finding is written, the Convener freezes a
**COVERAGE MAP**: every facet of the declared surface owned by **exactly one seat**, `unfiled = 0` and
`double-owned = 0`. An **unowned partition is a finding, not a silent skip**; cold/inventory-only surfaces are
**NAMED as inventory-only**, never quietly dropped. "Coverage-complete" is *counted*, not vibed. *(Sole canonical
statement; §0 N0, §2, §5 cite it, never restate.)*

The sweep runs **BFS**: all seats file in **PARALLEL**, each in its own **deliverable shape** so a seat with
nothing real to say produces a **visibly empty** path, not a checkbox: appsec → `source → sink`; authz →
`precondition → asset`; supply-chain → `dep → build → deploy`; decision → `option × criteria` cells; research →
`≥2-independent-lineage` claims. Seats **do not read each other mid-sweep** (frozen artifacts, no lateral comms).

Where the domain supports it, a **CHAIN-COMPOSITION** pass follows - **a conditional PASS, not a standing role
and not a rank** (§1/§8 anti-ratchet forbids a fourth counted seat). It inherits the **§1 inherited model-where-judgment
floor**, is by construction **exempt** from the §2 no-peer-evidence tooth (composing peers' frozen findings *is*
its job), and re-reads every seat's MED findings to compose them - three MEDs (exposed metadata + over-scoped
role + unlogged egress) can be one HIGH kill-chain no single seat sees. **A composed HIGH takes its one
different-lens cross-examiner INSIDE the single §4 round** (its first challenge - no §8 recursion), and
**composed findings re-enter the §6 class match like any finding.** Composition does not smuggle an unchallenged
HIGH into the report.

Each memo is a bare claim `{claim_id, statement, evidence[], tag, facet}` - one falsifiable assertion, a
resolvable pointer, an honest tag (§Kernel), no confidence/narrative field.

## §4 - VERIFY: DIFFERENT-LENS PEER CROSS-EXAMINATION - the light-but-real tooth

**Severity ladder (inherited from Nuclear §3, by reference): `max`{impact, reachability, reversibility}** - HIGH
· MED-HIGH · MED · LOW-MED/LOW. **S_block = MED-HIGH** - the cross-exam / block threshold; N1's
"cross-exam-complete" is arithmetic.

Every finding **≥ S_block** is assigned **exactly ONE cross-examiner from a provably different lens**
(`lens(examiner) ≠ lens(author)`, paired against the peer whose objective function most **conflicts**). The
examiner does **two cheap-but-real things** and may **not** rubber-stamp:

**(a) Independently re-resolve the provenance pointer, first-hand from disk** - the cross-exam memo **must carry
the challenger's OWN evidence slice** (distinct quoted bytes / an independent locator / a re-derived value that
is **not a copy of the author's pointer**). **Echoing the author's pointer is a VOID concur that does not bank.**

**(b) For any HIGH finding**, either **NAME the concrete TRIGGER** or **break ONE load-bearing link** -
reachability (precondition), impact (is the sink/asset actually dangerous), or a **pointed** compensating control
(an unpointed "there's a check upstream" is **not** a rebuttal).

**(c) DECOMPOSITION CHALLENGE (v1.1 - closes the wrong-together hole the frame-critic named):** **at least one
cross-exam per sweep must challenge the DECOMPOSITION, not only the value tradeoff** - name a threat that could
fall *between* the frozen facets (a gap the partition itself hides). A different-lens challenge normally probes
the tradeoff (perf vs maintainability, security vs API); this clause forces one to probe the partition. A named
between-facet threat re-opens the coverage map (LAW-PARTITION) as a finding.

**LAW-NOLAUNDER (auto-downgrade):** a finding whose pointer will not re-resolve or whose trigger cannot be named
is **auto-downgraded, never laundered upward** (HIGH → advisory; REACHABLE-PATH-SHOWN → THEORETICAL). *(Canonical
in §Kernel; cited here.)*

**Kept from Nuclear:** provenance reproduced by a **second independent head** + a **real failing condition** +
every challenge **written and frozen**. **Dropped, and why safe at Napalm stakes:** the redundant blind N-wave
and the requirement to write the failing test / build the negative control - that is Nuclear T1's job on
escalation, and its absence is itself the escalation trigger (§6). The honest stamp on every banked finding:
**`CONCURRED, not PROVEN`** (LAW-AUTHORITY, §6). Below S_block: single-head, honest-tagged, does not bank as
second-head-verified.

## §5 - CONVERGENCE = COVERAGE + CONSENSUS, dissent recorded verbatim

Napalm converges when the **surface is closed** and the **council concurs** - re-derivable by arithmetic
(LAW-COUNT), never label-trusted. Three conditions, all counted from disk:

- **COVERAGE-COMPLETE** - every facet filed; every seat's hunted-failure fired or recorded searched-and-empty (a
  silent seat is **not** coverage); no map region above threshold with zero seats (LAW-PARTITION).
- **CROSS-EXAM-COMPLETE** - every block-level finding has a different-lens challenge frozen on disk (own-evidence
  present, §4a), and **the sweep's decomposition-challenge (§4c) is on disk.**
- **CONSENSUS** - every banked finding is `CONCURRED` with its pointer independently re-resolved.

**Dissent is a DELIVERABLE.** Every council **SPLIT** and every **seat-owned KILL** (a seat's hard reject of a
claim on its exclusive facet, not shared by the council) rides **verbatim** to the owner in a **DISSENT LEDGER** -
**SPLITs tagged escalate-to-Nuclear; seat-owned KILLs tagged documented-open** - never averaged into a mush.
**Coverage-complete always means the DECLARED surface, not the universe.** Where any condition is short, the
report prints **"surveyed, not saturated"** rather than "converged."

## §6 - ESCALATION + THE AUTHORITY CEILING

**Napalm cannot drill; it escalates.** A finding is handed UP on **either** trigger:

1. **SPLIT** - after one cross-exam the pair cannot reconcile **on the evidence** (a genuine truth-conflict). A
   split is the **PRODUCT of a BFS sweep, not a failure** - only DFS depth settles it.
2. **HIGH-DEEP** - the finding is HIGH severity **AND** its done-bar needs **fail-on-demand proof**.

**MANDATORY-escalate trigger (re-derived at N1 by class/keyword match, fail-closed; the two judgment-shaped
members - compensating-control and one-way-door - round toward ESCALATE on ambiguity and are independently forced
up by SPLIT / HIGH-DEEP / the Specced authority ceiling):** any finding tagged with a claim in this set **MUST**
carry an escalation packet, or **N1 fails closed** - `RCE · auth/authz bypass · tenant-isolation break ·
mass-exfil · crypto/signature break · data-loss · money/PII correctness · a load-bearing compensating-control
claim that must be proven to fail on demand · a cryptographic-provenance / supply-chain claim · a one-way-door
decision whose decisive fact is Specced-only or contested.`

**Mechanism (granted by the level above, never self-granted):** the Synthesizer rules `ESCALATE` and freezes an
**escalation PACKET = finding + both seats' memos + the cross-exam records.** That packet **IS a Nuclear
control-pass gap set** (1–3 named gaps → **Nuclear's control-pass door - Nuclear re-derives the tier (T1 or T2)
independently** and stands up **FRESH**). **Napalm never self-promotes** - it hands a frozen packet and keeps
sweeping in parallel; the proven verdict folds back and re-ranks.

**LAW-AUTHORITY (the ceiling) - canonical home.** Napalm writes `REACHABLE-PATH-SHOWN / CORROBORATED / CONCURRED`
on its own authority; **only Nuclear certifies** `EXPLOITED / ESTABLISHED-to-bedrock / compensating-control-
verified / one-way-door-PROVEN`. Its **one artifact is the `CONCURRED, not PROVEN` stamp** on every finding
Napalm banks. **Napalm never executes** - READ-ONLY; any done-bar needing `Built`/`Verified` escalates. Napalm's
code-ladder ceiling is **Specced** (`Built` only by relaying a **cited, resolvable static-check quote - reported,
never certified**; `Verified` is Nuclear's alone). *(Sole canonical statement; §4, §5, §9, §Defaults, §Honesty
cite it, never restate - resolving the §4-vs-§9 stamp-scope drift.)*

## §7 - GATES N0/N1/N2 + TERMINATION - boolean, re-derived from disk

**LAW-COUNT (count-from-disk) - canonical home.** Every gate condition is a **count or pointer re-derived from a
frozen artifact**; a recorded label is never trusted, fail-closed on unre-derivable (INV-C2/C12 inherited).
*(Sole canonical statement; §0, §5, N0–N2 cite it, never restate.)*

**INWARD RE-DERIVATION LAW (INV-C10-e, inherited inward - a generalization of provenance-or-die onto Napalm's
OWN instruments, NOT a new subsystem):** a Napalm gate count that would read the same with its **measured side
absent is VOID.** The coverage-map counter (`unfiled`/`double-owned`), the N1-b non-echoing own-evidence count,
the roster-selector's distinct-lens count, and the §6 class matcher each **must move when their measured input is
removed** - a count that cannot is not gating, and its PASS does not bank. Napalm proves this **by re-derivation
from disk only, never by an executed fail-on-demand control** (READ-ONLY, Specced ceiling - banking a transcript
would re-bolt a dropped tooth). *(Cited by N0/N1/N2; the frame-critic and the fail-closed GATE-ERROR are the only
other instrument checks Napalm runs.)*

Three gates, each condition backed by a **count or pointer**, **fail-closed** if unre-derivable (GATE-ERROR).

```
N0 CONVENE (opens the sweep) · auto+report:
   control-pass exists · Napalm route justified on the breadth×stakes 2×2 (LAW-COUNT) ·
   coverage map complete (LAW-PARTITION: every facet→one seat, unfiled=0, double-owned=0; each facet binds a
     control-pass gap or template lens) · cross-exam pairing complete (every seat→≥1 different-lens examiner) ·
   frame-critic spawned (subject+mission only) OR owner signed the coverage map · budget declared (INV-C11)

N1 SWEEP-EXIT (opens the report) · notify · PASS|FAIL|BLOCKED:
   (a) coverage-complete (every facet filed; every hunted-failure fired or searched-and-empty)
   (b) cross-exam-complete: FOR EACH block-level finding, count(cross-exam memos with a present, non-echoing
       own-evidence field) ≥ 1  (LAW-COUNT · INV-C10-e: the count must move when a memo's own-evidence is removed)
   (c) every banked finding CONCURRED with pointer independently re-resolved · the §4c decomposition-challenge on disk
   (d) every SPLIT / HIGH-DEEP / MANDATORY-escalate-class finding has a frozen escalation packet   ← fail-closed
   (e) the frame-critic's partition objection (if filed) carries a recorded disposition - owned / escalated /
       owner-waived-on-record - never merely spawned+printed
   (f) stop_reason re-derived from disk · round-count ≤ 2 · spend ≤ budget (INV-C11)
   escalation-fraction computed → notify if > 40% (mis-route, below)

N2 REPORT (closes the op) · HARD-STOP-owner:
   honest report delivered · every pointer re-resolves (re-resolved by the Convener - count + compare, never
   classify) · "CONCURRED, not PROVEN" caveat present · escalation packets listed · frame-check verbatim ·
   "swept-with-splits"/"surveyed-not-saturated" banner where applicable · owner countersign
```

**TERMINATION - a closed stop_reason set** (no oracle-dry, no cap-by-exhaustion - those are depth stops):
`swept` (coverage + cross-exam + consensus green) · `swept-with-splits` (coverage complete but ≥1 finding
escalated/split - prints `council split on N findings, escalated to Nuclear`) · `scoped` (a pre-declared bounded
surface covered) · `owner-halt` (see §Defaults).

Every stop writes **`CONVERGENCE-DECISION`** (stop_reason · coverage tally · dissent ledger · escalation packets
· **escalation fraction** · budget spent vs declared). **If escalations exceed the declared threshold (default
> 40% of block findings), print a `G0 MIS-ROUTE: escalation fraction exceeded threshold - surface is likely
narrow+expensive OR pervasively mandatory-class; owner re-decides the route` banner** - the proportionality
mirror of §0's 2×2, routing an over-escalating Napalm to an **owner re-decision**, not celebrating it as
"swept-with-splits."

## §8 - PROPORTIONALITY + ANTI-RATCHET: Napalm earns its lightness

**LAW-RATCHET (addition-burden) - canonical home.** De-escalation is free; **addition carries the burden of
proof** - every added seat / finer facet / extra pass must name what it uniquely owns, and depth routes to
Nuclear, never to a Napalm round. *(Sole canonical statement; §2, §3, §4, §6 cite it, never restate.)*

The anti-ratchet spine is **structural:** **Napalm CANNOT drill by construction.** Budget `{max_seats, max_bytes
(byte-sentinel armed), max_rounds = 1 sweep + 1 cross-exam}`. **SINGLE-SWEEP by default.** A second cross-exam
pass is **not a route-back - it is an ESCALATION;** cross-exam **does not recurse.** Proportionality is proven at
N0 (a one-facet subject is a **T0 do-it**). The **mirror error is guarded too:** **under-escalating** a
HIGH-stakes or one-way-door finding to save a round is a **violation** (§6). The scope firewall parks
machinery-about-Napalm findings in a backlog. In one line: **Napalm goes a mile wide and refuses to also go a
mile deep - depth is Nuclear's, always via a frozen handoff.**

## §9 - THE REPORT: one honest deliverable

Napalm delivers **exactly one report.** Analysis that does not converge to a **usable map** (or, for decision
subjects, a **ranked pick**) is a **non-deliverable** - pick-avoidance is the input, not the output. The report
carries:

1. subject / fence + the **G0 route justification** (the breadth × stakes arithmetic);
2. the **frozen COVERAGE MAP** - every facet's owner + every inventory-only / unswept partition **named**;
3. **per-seat findings by class**, each with its resolvable pointer + its **honest tag** (never laundered,
   LAW-NOLAUNDER);
4. the **cross-exam outcome** per finding - one of `CONCURRED` (banks) / `narrowed` (downgraded) / `void` (echoed
   pointer, does not bank, §4a) - with the challenger's **lens named**, plus the **§4c decomposition-challenge**;
5. the **DISSENT LEDGER** verbatim (SPLITs escalate-to-Nuclear · seat-owned KILLs documented-open);
6. the **frame-check** verbatim (appendix - raw) + its **N1 disposition**;
7. the **ESCALATION PACKETS** listed with their Nuclear-strike status + the **escalation fraction**;
8. the `stop_reason` + **CONVERGENCE-DECISION** + budget spent vs declared;
9. the mandatory **RESIDUAL** - the declared-surface caveat, the inventory-only facets, and the **`CONCURRED, not
   PROVEN`** stamp (LAW-AUTHORITY) on everything Napalm certified on its own authority;
10. the **`swept, not saturated` / `swept-with-splits`** banner wherever coverage/cross-exam/consensus fell short.

## §Kernel - WHAT NAPALM KEEPS FROM NUCLEAR (truth+verify), and WHAT IT DROPS

**KEPT (the truth+verify kernel):**
- **Provenance-or-die** - every finding carries a resolvable pointer; a pointer that will not resolve downgrades
  or discards it. Enforced at **both ends** for block-level findings.
- **Honest tags - Specced ≠ Built ≠ Verified**, unlaundered, worn as a domain skin (`THEORETICAL /
  REACHABLE-PATH-SHOWN / EXPLOITED`; `ASSERTED / CORROBORATED / ESTABLISHED`; `Specced / Built / Verified`).
  **LAW-NOLAUNDER (auto-downgrade) - canonical home:** no tag is promoted a rung it did not earn; a finding whose
  pointer will not re-resolve or whose trigger cannot be named is **auto-downgraded, never laundered upward.**
  *(Sole canonical statement; §4, §6, §9 cite it, never restate.)*
- **Frozen artifacts + no lateral comms**; **write-fence** - the council writes ONLY under its **Napalm op home =
  `<project>/napalm/<op-slug>/`** (mirroring Nuclear §0.5's `nuclear/` tree); the subject is **READ-ONLY.**
- **A delivered honest report** (§9); **proportionate G0-style entry** (§0); **gates re-derived from disk**
  (LAW-COUNT); **the orchestrator invariant** (§1); **budget-as-gate-currency** (INV-C11).

**DROPPED, and why safe at Napalm stakes:**
- **Blind refuter waves** → **one** named, non-blind, **different-lens** challenger; the anchoring residual is
  bought back by the mandatory independent pointer re-resolution + provably-different lens + the §4c
  decomposition-challenge (§4).
- **The universal fail-on-demand negative control** → dropped for every finding Napalm certifies on its own
  authority; its absence is the escalation trigger (§6). **Dropping it also drops its inward clause (Nuclear
  C10-e): Napalm's own gate instruments - coverage-map counter, N1-b count, roster selector, class matcher - are
  held to the VOID-if-unmoved re-derivation law (§7) but are never proven able to fail by an executed control;**
  the frame-critic and the fail-closed GATE-ERROR are the only instrument checks.
- **Oracle-dry exhaustion / multi-round drilling** → coverage-complete + consensus, single-sweep.
- **The heavy T2 formation** → three ranks + two roles (+ the conditional chain-composition pass). The
  frame-critic is **kept as a role** (conflicting lenses inside a Convener-drawn partition cannot question the
  partition; §4c + the N1 disposition close what remains).

## §Honesty Ledger

Record only current evidence, the actual degree of reviewer independence, dissent, missing checks, and remaining uncertainty. A council consensus is CONCURRED, not PROVEN.
